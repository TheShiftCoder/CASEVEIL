## 2. Victim Profile
- **Name**: Adrian Blackwood
- **Age**: 68
- **Occupation**: Wealthy art collector, philanthropist
- **Personality**: Imperious, secretive, controlling, unforgiving
- **Cause of Death**: Poisoning (rare, fast-acting alkaloid — aconitine derivative) administered in a nightcap of whisky.
- **Time of Death**: 10:13 PM (exact; clock stopped at 10:20 PM when it was knocked over during struggle/staging).
- **Body Found**: 10:47 PM by Margaret Shaw (housekeeper) in the private study.

---

## 3. The Truth (Hidden from Player)

### Culprit
**Thomas Reed** (Personal Secretary)

### Motive
Adrian discovered Thomas had been systematically embezzling from the Blackwood estate and selling forgeries through the collection. Adrian planned to expose him at a meeting scheduled for the next morning and had already contacted his lawyer to rewrite the will, cutting Thomas out of a "loyal service" bequest.

### Method
Thomas prepared Adrian's customary evening whisky with a dissolved aconitine derivative (accessed via his academic background in chemistry before becoming a secretary). He entered the study at ~10:10 PM under the pretense of delivering the nightcap and "final correspondence." Adrian drank it, collapsed by 10:13 PM. Thomas staged the scene: knocked over the clock (stopping it at 10:20), broke a glass to suggest a struggle, and left via the study door, locking it from the outside with the key he palmed.

### Opportunity
- Thomas had legitimate access to the study at all hours.
- He prepared the nightcap as part of his routine.
- The storm masked sounds and limited movement.
- Margaret Shaw saw "someone" near the study at ~10:05 PM but could not identify them (she assumed it was Daniel).

### Cover Story
Thomas claims he was in the office (adjacent to the study) organizing correspondence from 9:30 PM onward. He says he heard nothing unusual. He "discovered" the body when Margaret screamed.

### Why the Body Was Found at 10:47 PM
Thomas needed time to return to the office, compose himself, and create an alibi trail (emails timestamped 10:15, 10:25). Margaret's routine check was at 10:45 PM; she found the locked study, got the spare key, and entered at 10:47 PM.

## 4. Suspects (5 Total)

### SUSPECT 1 — Eleanor Blackwood (Wife)
- **Age**: 48
- **Role**: Art patron, socialite
- **Personality**: Controlled, elegant, guarded, privately ruthless
- **Public Statement**: "I was in the conservatory tending to the orchids from 9:00 PM until Margaret called me. Adrian and I had dined at 8:30. He retired to his study. We were not on speaking terms."
- **Public Motive**: Inheritance (primary beneficiary of current will).
- **Hidden Motive**: None for murder. She wanted the will changed *in her favor*, not Adrian dead.
- **Secret**: She knew Adrian planned to change the will *against* her (leaving the bulk to a foundation). She confronted him at 9:15 PM. It was heated. She left angry.
- **Alibi**: Conservatory. No witness. Security camera in conservatory was disabled by storm.
- **Actual Movements**: Conservatory 9:00–10:40 PM. Heard nothing.
- **Knowledge of Crime**: None. Suspects Daniel.
- **Behavioral Traits**: Composure under pressure; answers precisely; avoids volunteering information.
- **Contradictions**: Claims she hadn't spoken to Adrian after 8:30 PM, but the argument at 9:15 PM is corroborated by Margaret (heard raised voices).
- **Interview Triggers**:
  - Will → Reveals argument.
  - Poison → Denies knowledge, suggests "Adrian had enemies."
  - Thomas → "Efficient. Too efficient. He knew everything."
- **Evidence Reactions**:
  - Presented: Draft Will (unsigned) → Admits argument, denies murder.

### SUSPECT 2 — Thomas Reed (Personal Secretary) — **THE CULPRIT**
- **Age**: 36
- **Role**: Personal secretary, confidant, gatekeeper
- **Personality**: Nervous precision, defensive, overly helpful, masks intelligence
- **Public Statement**: "I was in the office organizing the Q4 correspondence. The storm played havoc with the scanner. I never left the room. I heard nothing from the study — the walls are thick."
- **Public Motive**: "Loyal service" bequest in current will ($500k + art pieces).
- **Hidden Motive**: Embezzlement (~$2.3M over 4 years) + forgery sales. Adrian discovered it 3 days ago. Confrontation scheduled for 9:00 AM next day.
- **Secret**: Altered a provenance document for a forged painting (The Ravelle) to secure a private sale. The original document is in the study desk. He also accessed aconitine via a university contact.
- **Alibi**: Office (adjacent to study). No witness. Claims emails sent at 10:15, 10:25, 10:35 prove presence.
- **Actual Movements**: Office 9:30–10:05 PM. Study 10:05–10:13 PM (murder). Office 10:13–10:47 PM (staging alibi).
- **Knowledge of Crime**: Complete.
- **Behavioral Traits**: Over-explains; volunteers alibi details unprompted; fidgets when pressed on timing; uses "I don't recall" strategically.
- **Contradictions**:
  1. Email timestamps show activity, but metadata reveals they were scheduled/sent via script (discovered via forensic analysis of evidence).
  2. Claims he never entered the study, but his fingerprints are on the nightcap glass (only partial — he wore gloves but missed the base).
  3. Says he heard nothing, but the clock chime at 10:00 PM is audible from the office.
- **Interview Triggers**:
  - Financial records → Nervous, deflects to "Adrian trusted me completely."
  - The Ravelle painting → "A masterpiece. Tragic if its provenance were questioned."
  - Poison → "I'm a secretary, not a chemist."
  - Clock/Time → Insists he was in the office "the whole time."
- **Evidence Reactions**:

  - Presented: Forged provenance → Long pause. "Where did you get that? That's... privileged."
  - Presented: Poison vial → Visible shock (micro-expression). "This is absurd."
  - Presented: Nightcap glass (fingerprint) → "I... brought him the drink. Yes. At 9:45. As always."

### SUSPECT 3 — Clara Whitmore (Family Friend / Art Historian)
- **Age**: 32
- **Role**: Art historian, authentication consultant
- **Personality**: Intelligent, observant, evasive, intellectually arrogant
- **Public Statement**: "I left the study before dinner, around 8:45 PM. I spent the evening in the gallery reviewing the new acquisition. I saw no one."
- **Public Motive**: Professional reputation; access to collection.
- **Hidden Motive**: She authenticated the forged Ravelle painting for Thomas (unknowingly — she was given falsified research). She fears exposure will ruin her career. She also stood to gain a curator position at a major museum contingent on the Blackwood collection's integrity.
- **Secret**: She discovered an anomaly in the Ravelle's brushwork *that evening* at 9:00 PM in the gallery and confronted Thomas at 9:20 PM in the hallway. He dismissed her. She did not tell Adrian.
- **Alibi**: Gallery. No witness. Security log shows gallery door badge-in at 8:52 PM, no badge-out until 10:50 PM.
- **Actual Movements**: Gallery 8:52 PM onward. Hallway confrontation with Thomas at 9:20 PM.
- **Knowledge of Crime**: Suspects forgery, not murder. Realizes Thomas is dangerous.
- **Behavioral Traits**: Answers questions with questions; redirects to evidence; calm but eyes dart.
- **Contradictions**: Claims she "saw no one," but Margaret saw her in the hallway at 9:20 PM arguing with Thomas.
- **Interview Triggers**:
  - The Ravelle → "A controversial piece. The provenance is... complex."
  - Thomas → "Ambitious. Protective of his position."
  - Forgery → "Authentication is rarely black and white."
  - Gallery → "I was working. The light is best at night."
- **Evidence Reactions**:
  - Presented: Forged provenance → "This... this isn't the research I was given. Thomas provided the documentation."
  - Presented: Poison → "I study art, not toxins."
  - Presented: Nightcap glass → No reaction.

- **Age**: 27
- **Role**: Failed entrepreneur, perpetual debtor

- **Public Statement**: "I was in the billiard room. Drinking. Alone. As usual. Ask the bottle."

- **Hidden Motive**: Desperation. He stole a letter from Adrian's desk at 9:40 PM (proof of Thomas's embezzlement) hoping to blackmail *Thomas*, not Adrian. He doesn't know the letter's full significance.
- **Secret**: He has the stolen letter (hidden in his coat pocket). He was in the study at 9:40 PM (uninvited, door was unlocked). He hid when he heard footsteps (Thomas at 10:05 PM).
- **Alibi**: Billiard room. No witness. Bottle confirms consumption.
- **Actual Movements**: Billiard room 8:30–9:30 PM. Study 9:40–9:55 PM (steals letter, hides in closet). Billiard room 9:55 PM onward.
- **Knowledge of Crime**: Heard Thomas enter at 10:05 PM, heard a glass clink, heard a thud. Did not see the murder. Terrified.
- **Behavioral Traits**: Hostile to authority; lies about small things; cracks under sustained pressure.
- **Contradictions**: Claims he never entered the study, but his fingerprint is on the desk drawer (from 9:40 PM).
- **Interview Triggers**:
  - Money/Debt → "Uncle was a bastard. He liked watching me beg."
  - Letter → Panic. "I don't know what you're talking about."

- **Secret**: She has the spare key to the study. She found the poison vial in Thomas's wastebasket at 6:00 AM (next morning) and hid it in her room, unsure what to do. She also knows Adrian was planning to fire Thomas *and* change the will.
- **Alibi**: Kitchen/hallway. Corroborated by no one but consistent.
- **Actual Movements**: Kitchen 9:00–10:45 PM. Hallway 10:45 PM (heard thud earlier at ~10:13 but dismissed it as storm). Study 10:47 PM (discovery).
- **Knowledge of Crime**: Partial. Knows Thomas is lying. Knows Daniel was in the study. Knows Clara confronted Thomas.
- **Behavioral Traits**: Speaks in measured tones; pauses before answering; never lies directly but omits strategically.
- **Contradictions**: Claims she "heard nothing," but admits to Daniel's movement at 9:55 PM and Clara/Thomas at 9:20 PM when pressed.
- **Interview Triggers**:

  - Daniel → "Troubled. The master worried for him."
  - Clara → "Sharp mind. She saw things."
